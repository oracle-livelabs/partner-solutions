# CrimeData Agentic Framework Roadmap

## Purpose

Support authorised investigators to build a more complete, lawful and evidence-grounded case picture. The framework assists investigative thinking; it does not determine responsibility, credibility, risk, enforcement action, or case outcomes.

## V1 — Evidence & Case-Readiness

**Status:** implemented as a review-only synthetic-data journey.

- Select an authorised watched-pattern scenario.
- Assemble linked incident, report and evidence-metadata coverage.
- Surface count-based gaps explicitly.
- Present training procedure context and investigator questions.
- Log the preparation event in the Oracle JSON audit trail.
- Require human review; no external or operational action is available.

Before operational use, replace the training guidance with an approved, versioned procedure and disclosure library with source citations.

## V2 — Controlled case collaboration

- Authorised investigator task assignment, handover and approval workflow.
- Role- and case-scoped access controls.
- Evidence provenance, conflict and disclosure-review checkpoints.
- No autonomous task completion or operational instructions.

## V3 — Mobile small-group training experience

- Synthetic/training case rooms, shared clue boards and allocated review tasks.
- Team progress view and facilitator oversight.
- Begin with demonstration data only; evaluate governance before any live-data consideration.

## V4 — AI Ops and governance dashboard

- Tool usage, policy retrieval, audit exceptions, unanswered questions and review queues.
- Data-quality and workflow telemetry, not people scoring or crime prediction.
- Ongoing evaluation for scope compliance, uncertainty handling and human-review outcomes.

## Non-negotiable guardrails

- Evidence and records remain scoped through Deep Data Security.
- Every agent result distinguishes observed records, unverified material and gaps.
- No suspect ranking, guilt inference, credibility scoring, risk assessment, prediction, surveillance, arrest, charging or deployment recommendation.
- Human authority is required for all investigative and operational decisions.
