Source: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2.0.2/countries-110m.json
Natural Earth 4.1.0, 1:110m country boundaries; public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Converted from quantized TopoJSON to GeoJSON; coordinates rounded to four decimals.
Bundled locally; no uploaded travel data is sent to a map provider.
