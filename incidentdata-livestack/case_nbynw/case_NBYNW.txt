Operation Red DirtWe suspect George Kaplan, is using his Aboriginal art gallery in Alice Springs as a front to launder substantial sums of illicit funds.

 The gallery has attracted an unusual mix of high-value clients, including Philip Vandamm, a suspected international financier of organized crime, and Eve Kendall, an art consultant with links to defunct mining companies used in past fraud cases. 

Financial records reveal repeated transactions just below AUSTRAC’s reporting threshold, and artwork is being flipped at suspiciously inflated prices, suggesting classic placement-layering-integration laundering behaviour.

The presence of Clara Thornhill as co-director of the “Ochre East Foundation” – a nonprofit receiving large donations traced back to Kaplan’s clients – raises further concerns of charitable front misuse. 

The web of buyers, art assets, and shadow donors appears to be part of a broader laundering network potentially tied to corrupt officials and high-level business interests. 

Surveillance, Finance, Social and Travel data need to be gathered to confirm movement of goods and funds, and Kaplan’s, transaction trail, and link to Vandamm are now considered top investigative priorities.
