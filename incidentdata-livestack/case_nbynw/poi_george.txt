George Kaplan is suspected of using his Aboriginal art gallery in Alice Springs to launder proceeds of crime. The gallery has attracted high-value clients with known criminal or fraudulent associations, including Philip Vandamm (suspected international money launderer) and Eve Kendall (linked to defunct mining companies involved in past financial crimes).

Kaplan’s financial records reveal frequent transactions just below AUSTRAC's reportable threshold, and numerous artworks have been resold at inflated prices. He also co-directs a nonprofit, Ochre East Foundation, with Clara Thornhill. The foundation has received large donations traced to Kaplan’s clients. These patterns indicate potential misuse of a charitable entity for money laundering. Kaplan’s associations, travel activity, and transaction trail are now a focus of active surveillance and financial investigation.
