▣ Data Room

From a ZIP of material to a reviewed case picture

Upload a bundle of mixed files (text, CSV, JSON, audio, photos). The Data Room processes it in seven stages and keeps every result in the database.

  1. LoadUpload and store the original archive
  2. UnzipCount and catalogue each file
  3. IngestStore each source with its checksum
  4. Identify & describeAI description of every file, with previews
  5. LearnBatch Brief that cites its sources
  6. ConnectVector and reference links, reviewed by an agent team
  7. PublishSaved review pack: JSON plus manifest, connections and timeline CSVs

Applies to every feature

Ground rules

Every claim has a source

AI output points to exact excerpts or rows. Anything unverified shows as “Not stated” or is left out with a warning.

AI output is a draft

Groupings, matches and connections need a person to review them. They don't establish identity or guilt.

No scoring or prediction

No suspect ranking, risk scores, predictions or recommended actions. Agents only read.

Everything is audited

Agent tool calls, review decisions and conversations are logged as JSON in the database.
