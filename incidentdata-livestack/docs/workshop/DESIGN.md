The learner starts with a synthetic Adelaide payment-document story, prepares the sample package in **Workshop & Guides**, then follows the requested page order. Scene 13 is omitted and **AI Opps** keeps its Scene 14 label.

The seven animations are conceptual teaching aids. Three reuse and adapt the earlier cut; four cover the watch graph and newly emphasised Ask, agent and Data Room journeys. The workshop uses 12 project illustrations rather than carrying Dublin screenshots into the Adelaide version.

The local source material confirms the nine saved city choices and the Data Room stage names. It also records a previous mismatch in Adelaide narrative-vector coverage. Current results, sample-package contents, exact fourth graph-view label and live AI service readiness need a prepared session check. The chapter wording makes those actions conditional and asks the presenter to record what is actually visible.
