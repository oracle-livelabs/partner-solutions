# The Care-Trust: Who knows?

Make the next investigative question obvious without making its answer inevitable. A learner should start at a watch pattern, follow a recorded lead, inspect the original incident, open its sources and return with context intact. Names, identifiers and expected conclusions must not have to be memorised.

## Work to the requested scope

- For a story or assessment, inspect and explain; do not load or deploy.
- For an authorised implementation, inspect the current active database profile and saved profile before writing. Amsterdam is this story's existing implementation, not permission to switch another active dataset or connect to a different database.
- Reuse the repository's versioned capture, validation, transaction and release scripts after checking their guards. Prior release scripts are receipts of a particular deployment, not general commands safe to replay.

Read [the continuity contract](references/continuity-contract.md) for data, navigation or capability work. Read [the research boundary](references/research-boundary.md) when adding authentic Dutch safeguarding characteristics; verify newer factual claims with primary sources.

## Essential decisions

1. Capture bounded fact cards for the chosen incidents, current posts, reviewed links, existing POI roles, evidence and resolved outcomes. Preserve them; add clearly authored supplements where appropriate. Do not retrofit an existing burglary narrative into a proved care-fraud allegation.
2. Write a short question-led story. Separate public concern, private attributed follow-up, documentary observation, analyst comparison and inference. Give every transition an explicit source or state that no relationship is recorded.
3. Include a plausible lawful explanation and a genuinely unresolved question. Care dependency is neither proof of abuse nor a reason to discredit the resident. Do not turn a care worker, family member or provider into an offender by occupation or association.
4. Implement continuity alongside the data: exact linked-incident retrieval, visible linkage basis, source titles, question-sized comparison sets, onward/back controls and user/profile-scoped state. Semantic ranking is a separate discovery action, never an implicit relationship.
5. Verify data and UI together, including a restricted persona, mismatched references, an unlinked post, counter-evidence and a return trip. A passing citation-reference check does not validate an AI interpretation. Keep evaluator answers outside indexed source text, runtime story prompts and AI tool payloads.

## Hand-off

Provide a short route starting with the watch-pattern name, what was actually loaded/deployed, what was tested and what remains uncertain. Distinguish native database capabilities exercised from illustrative or unimplemented ideas. Keep the detailed implementation receipts separate from the user's simple walkthrough.
